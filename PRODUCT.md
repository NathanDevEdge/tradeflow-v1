# TradeFlow — Product Context

## What it is
TradeFlow is an affordable ERP system built specifically for small businesses and sole traders. It helps them create quotes and purchase orders, manage suppliers and customers, and handle the day-to-day admin that currently lives in spreadsheets or expensive enterprise software.

## Mission
Help the little people — small business owners, sole traders, and operators — access genuinely good software that saves them time and lets them compete with larger businesses, without the enterprise price tag or the weeks of setup.

## Slogan
**"Better than Spreadsheets, Cheaper than the rest"**

## Target users
- Small business owners (1–20 employees)
- Sole traders
- Wholesale distribution businesses (fencing, building materials, outdoor living, trades)
- People currently using spreadsheets or pen-and-paper for quoting and ordering
- Non-technical users who need software that just works

## Core features
- Quote builder with real-time margin calculations
- Purchase order management with smart buy-price logic (pack vs loose pricing)
- Pricelist management (CSV upload)
- Customer and supplier contact management
- PDF generation for quotes and POs
- Multi-user team access with roles
- Company branding (logo, ABN, address in PDFs)

## Tone & personality
- Approachable and human — not corporate, not cold
- Confident but not arrogant
- Straightforward — no jargon, no bloat
- Feels like software built by someone who actually understands small business

## Design principles
- **Clarity over cleverness** — every screen should be instantly understandable
- **Speed** — users are busy; get them in and out fast
- **Trust** — numbers and data must feel accurate and reliable
- **Affordable feel without feeling cheap** — clean and professional, not enterprise-bloated
- **Mobile-aware** — many users will check things on their phone

## Anti-references (what to avoid)
- SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics — too complex, too cold, too corporate
- Clipart-heavy "SMB software" from the 2000s — dated and untrustworthy
- Overly playful / startup-cute — this is a business tool, not a consumer app

## Register
Product (app UI — design serves the product)
